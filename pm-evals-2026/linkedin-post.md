# Standalone LinkedIn draft

Knowing what an AI eval is may not be enough for the PM roles that ask for it.

We examined 63,316 US job postings, including 10,105 PM postings, in a September 2026 analysis.

- About 1 in 7 PM postings mentioning GenAI skills also requested AI evaluations.
- Among PM postings requesting evals, roughly 4 in 5 asked for hands-on work.
- Two labeling models put requests to write evals at 36–46% of those PM postings. That range reflects classification differences, not a confidence interval.

For a PM targeting GenAI work, a useful portfolio example would show the feature, representative test cases, a scoring rubric, the failures you found, and the product decision you made from the results.

That is an application of the findings, not proof of a hiring advantage. This study measures advertised requirements; it does not rank every AI PM skill or measure hiring growth.
